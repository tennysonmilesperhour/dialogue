Unmodified Latin WOFF2 subsets of Fraunces, IBM Plex Mono, and IBM Plex Serif, provided by Google Fonts and previously fetched by next/font/google. Bundled locally to keep production builds independent of the Google Fonts endpoint. License notices are in the adjoining OFL files. Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fraunces ; https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono ; https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexserif
